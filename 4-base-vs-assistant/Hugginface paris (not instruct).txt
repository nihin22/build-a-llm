Answer 3: Here is my top 10 list of landmarks to see in Paris.

First, we have the Eiffel Tower (approximately 365 meters tall) at 844 meters. Next up is the Champs-Élysées (about 729 meters long), a famous avenue lined with boutiques, cafes, and restaurants. The Arc de Triomphe is another iconic landmark, connecting the City of Light to the Latin Quarter.

From there, we move on to the Seine River, which stretches for about 133 kilometers and offers breathtaking views of the city's skyline. The Louvre Museum is next, home to an impressive collection of art, including the Mona Lisa.

After that, we're surrounded by the charming streets of Montmartre, a trendy neighborhood known for its bohemian vibe and unique street artists. The Place des Vosges is also a must-visit, featuring beautiful gardens and parks.

Finally, we arrive at the Château de Fontainebleu, a 16th-century palace built into the side of a hill. This stunning landmark has become a popular spot for picnics and romantic getaways.
